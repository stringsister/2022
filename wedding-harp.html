Wedding Harpist in Toronto

Creating unforgettable musical moments for your special day.

Imagine walking down the aisle to the ethereal, romantic sound of the harp. As a professional wedding harpist in Toronto, Naomi Jackson specializes in providing the perfect soundtrack for your love story. From the moment your guests arrive until the final recessional, the harp adds a layer of sophistication and magic that recorded music simply cannot match.

Elegant Ceremony Music

Your wedding ceremony is the heart of your day. We help you select pieces that resonate with your journey, performing live across top GTA venues like Casa Loma, Graydon Hall Manor, and The Estates of Sunnybrook.

  • The Processional: Timeless melodies for the grand bridal entrance.
  • Signing of the Register: Gentle background music for the legal signing.
  • The Recessional: An uplifting, celebratory exit as a newly married couple.

Cocktail Hour Ambiance

Transition seamlessly into your cocktail hour with a repertoire that blends contemporary pop hits, Disney classics, and jazz standards. The harp provides an ideal volume level—vibrant enough to set the mood, yet quiet enough for easy conversation.
