Who are the StringSisters?

Heather and Naomi are twin sisters who love to travel and perform music together! Over the past decade, they have performed in some of the GTA's most beautiful venues, including Koerner Hall, The ROM, Graydon Hall, and Casa Loma.

Naomi - The Harpist

Naomi Jackson is a classically trained harpist who professionally performs for special events and weddings. Beginning with Celtic and Folk genres, Naomi primarily studied Classical repertoire at the Conservatory following the RCM method. However, Naomi enjoys playing most genres, including Contemporary, Jazz, Film Scores, and Pop.

Naomi's musical background includes being a two-time gold medalist for the Royal Conservatory of Music's practical harp exams. Her teaching approach emphasizes musical theory and technique, personally tailoring each lesson to the student's goals and learning style.

Heather - The Cellist

Heather has studied the cello for nearly a decade, fascinated by the instrument's depth and warmth. Her appreciation for film scores led her to pursue the cello as the staple of modern cinematic themes. Apart from hundreds of weddings, Heather has been a part of music recordings, traveling orchestras, and performed with KYGO at VELD in 2019.

Heather offers cello lessons for all ages and backgrounds. Her focus is on the basics of the cello, encouraging good posture and technique while molding each class to the student's capabilities. While Classical and Celtic genres shaped her training, she enjoys exploring all musical styles with her students.
