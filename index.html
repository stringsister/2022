GTA & BEYOND

Live Music Across Ontario

Based in the Greater Toronto Area, we perform elegant live harp and cello music for weddings, corporate events, and special occasions throughout the GTA and across some of Ontario’s most beautiful destinations.

Greater Toronto Area

Toronto & the GTA

We regularly perform throughout Toronto and the surrounding GTA, including beautiful local wedding venues such as Madison Greenhouse in Newmarket, Three Feathers Terrace in Lefroy, and The Arlington Estate in Vaughan. We're also frequently booked in Aurora, King City, Richmond Hill, and Markham.
