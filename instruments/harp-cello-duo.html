The StringSisters

Harp & Cello Duo in Toronto & the GTA

Naomi on harp and Heather on cello are sisters who perform together as a duo for weddings, corporate events and private celebrations across the GTA — a fuller, more dynamic sound than either instrument alone, at a reduced rate when booked together.

Two instruments, one sound

Harp and cello sit at opposite ends of the range, and that's exactly why they work so well together. Cello brings a warm, resonant low end — the foundation, the heartbeat of a piece. Harp brings shimmer, melody and light on top of it. Played solo, each is beautiful on its own terms. Played together, they cover the full range of an orchestra in miniature, and the result is fuller, more emotional and far more dynamic than either instrument alone.

Built for a full day, not a single moment

A duo can build and release tension in a way a single instrument can't — quiet and intimate for a processional, then rich and full-bodied for cocktail hour and reception, without ever needing a break or a backing track. Naomi and Heather tailor each set to the couple or event, blending classical repertoire with modern arrangements, and adjusting the balance between harp and cello depending on the room, the moment and the mood.

Sisters who genuinely love playing together

The StringSisters isn't two musicians who happened to get booked on the same day — it's two sisters who love making music together, and it shows. Naomi on harp and Heather on cello have spent years learning how to listen to each other, trade melody and support seamlessly, and read a room as a team rather than as two separate acts sharing a stage. Couples and event planners consistently tell us that connection is what makes the duo feel special, not just fuller.

A reduced rate for booking us together

Because Naomi and Heather already perform together regularly and travel to events as a pair, we're able to offer a reduced bundled rate for the harp & cello duo, compared to booking each of us as separate solo musicians. It's our way of making the fuller sound an easy decision — more music, more range, and two performers who already know how to play beautifully together, for less than the sum of two solo bookings. Ask us for a duo quote and we'll lay out the savings alongside solo pricing.

Weddings, corporate events and galas

The duo works beautifully for wedding ceremonies and receptions, and just as well for corporate receptions, product launches and galas where a longer run of show benefits from a fuller sound. Naomi and Heather have performed at Toronto landmarks including the ROM, Casa Loma and Koerner Hall.
