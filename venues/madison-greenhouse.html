Featured Venue · Newmarket

Live Music at Madison Greenhouse

Madison Greenhouse is a working greenhouse turned wedding venue — four acres of tropical plants, an indoor waterfall, and twinkling lights against the glass roof at night. If you're getting married there, here's how a live harp & cello duo fits into the day.

160 Bayview Parkway, Newmarket, ON

A greenhouse built for atmosphere

Madison Greenhouse isn't a banquet hall dressed up as a garden — it's an actual greenhouse, home to live tropical trees, cascading vines and an indoor waterfall, on four acres of landscaped grounds in Newmarket. The whole space does a lot of the decorating on its own, which leaves more room for the music and the moment to carry the day rather than competing with the room.

Where live music fits at Madison Greenhouse

The property is laid out in distinct spaces, each suited to a different part of the day.

Ceremony

Garden Chapel or Ivy Chapel

The outdoor Garden Chapel, with its cobblestone path and forest backdrop, suits an acoustic harp and cello setup beautifully. The indoor Ivy Chapel and Cocktail Room offers a covered alternative, surrounded by the same greenery.

Cocktail Hour

The Terrace

A partially covered patio strung with twinkle lights — a natural spot for us to set up while guests cross the wooden bridge from the ceremony.

Reception

The Fig Leaf Room

The main hall, with its indoor waterfall and dance floor, is where background music through dinner can rise into a live entrance or first-dance arrangement.

Why harp & cello suit this greenhouse

A tropical, greenery-filled room calls for instruments that can sit inside that atmosphere rather than cut against it — harp and cello both have a natural, unamplified warmth that pairs well with a space already doing so much of the visual work. One practical note: Madison Greenhouse doesn't run air conditioning, to protect the plants, so the room can run warm, especially in summer. We plan our setup and tuning around that, so the instruments sound their best whether your wedding is a July afternoon or a December evening under the twinkle lights.
