Featured Venue · Innisfil

Live Music at Three Feathers Terrace

Three Feathers Terrace is a four-season wedding venue just north of the city — a circular Moon Gate for your ceremony, a stone fireplace patio for cocktail hour, and a retractable-door Terrace reception space lit with chandeliers and grey timber beams. If you're getting married there, here's how a live harp & cello duo fits into the day.

1263 3rd Line, Innisfil, ON

A newer venue built for every season

Three Feathers Terrace is one of the newer wedding venues north of Toronto, and it's built around the idea that a wedding shouldn't have to choose between indoors and out. The reception space is wrapped in retractable doors, so the room can feel like an open-air terrace on a June evening or a fully enclosed, chandelier-lit hall in the middle of winter. That flexibility carries through the whole property, from a circular Moon Gate for outdoor vows to a stone fireplace patio for cocktail hour.

Where live music fits at Three Feathers Terrace

The property is laid out in distinct spaces, each suited to a different part of the day.

Ceremony

The Moon Gate

The circular Moon Gate, set among greenery on the grounds, suits an acoustic harp and cello setup beautifully. Three Feathers Terrace also offers an indoor ceremony option, so the same setup travels easily if the day calls for a plan B.

Cocktail Hour

The Patio

An outdoor patio built around a classic stone fireplace, with comfortable seating — a natural, relaxed spot for us to set up while guests move from the ceremony toward cocktails.

Reception

The Terrace

The four-season reception space, with its retractable doors, timber beams, and chandeliers overhead, is where background music through dinner can rise into a live entrance or first-dance arrangement.

  • Four-season retractable-door Terrace
  • Moon Gate outdoor ceremony
  • Indoor ceremony option
  • Stone fireplace patio
  • 100-guest minimum, May–Oct weekends

Why harp & cello suit this venue

Three Feathers Terrace does a lot with light and openness — big windows, grey timber beams, chandeliers strung overhead — and it calls for instruments that can hold their own in a room that size without ever needing to compete with it. Harp and cello both carry a natural, unamplified warmth that fills the Terrace comfortably, whether the retractable doors are thrown open on a summer evening or closed against a winter chill. We plan our warm-up and tuning around the season, so the instruments sound their best whether your wedding is a June afternoon on an open patio or a January evening indoors by the fireplace.
